Privacy Policy for Calenderp

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Calenderp ("the app," "we," "us") is a calendar app. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: almost everything stays on your device, and anything that is shared is end-to-end encrypted so we cannot read it.

Information stored on your device

Your calendars, events, tasks, settings, and custom icons are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your phone and is not sent to us unless you choose to share a calendar (see below). Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

Calendar access

If you choose to import from your device's calendar, the app reads your device calendar events only at that moment and only to copy them into Calenderp. The app does not continuously monitor or upload your device calendar.

Importing from a URL

If you import a calendar from a web address (.ics link) you provide, the app downloads that file over a secure (HTTPS) connection to import its events. The address you enter is used only to fetch that calendar.

Shared Calendars and our servers

If you use the Shared Calendars feature, the app connects to our backend provider (Supabase) to sync shared data between members. For this feature:

  • No account or personal details. Sign-in is anonymous — we do not collect your name, email, or a password. The app is assigned an anonymous identifier.
  • End-to-end encryption. Event details and calendar names are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded. Our servers store only the encrypted data and cannot read your event contents or calendar names.
  • What the server stores: encrypted event and calendar data, anonymous member identifiers, public encryption keys, and calendar membership and invitation information needed to deliver sharing.

Notifications

The app can show reminders and sharing notifications. These are generated on your device.

What we do not do

We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, we do not sell or share your data with advertisers, and we do not require a personal account.

Third-party services

Shared Calendars data is processed and hosted by Supabase, acting as our service provider. See Supabase's privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.

Data retention and deletion

Local data is removed when you uninstall the app. For Shared Calendars, you can leave or delete a shared calendar to remove your participation; to request deletion of encrypted shared data associated with your anonymous identifier, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Calenderp is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the effective date above when we do.
